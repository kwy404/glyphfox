Variable icons

One icon. Every weight.

Every icon renders along three axes: weight from 1 to 3 with optical compensation at small sizes, round or sharp corners, and outline, duotone or solid style generated from its closed shapes.

Morph engine

Anything into anything.

Paths are normalized, resampled, matched and interpolated, so any icon turns into any other. Hand tuned pairs make the everyday ones feel designed.

    Living states

    Icons that know how to move.

    Presets that understand the icon: draw traces the strokes in order, shake swings from the top like a bell. Trigger them on mount, hover, click or a state prop. Reduced motion is always respected.

    Modifiers

    Badges that cut their own gap.

    Add plus, check, alert, lock, clock, sync and more in any corner. The icon underneath is trimmed around the badge, so a few hundred icons give thousands of clean combinations.

    Fusion

    Two icons. One new glyph.

    Fuse 2 to 10 icons with seven layouts. Parts are scaled and snapped to the grid, and where they overlap the one behind is cut around the one in front, so lines never collide and the result still passes the lint.
