interface

Toggle left icon

Toggle left is an original glyphfox icon from the interface set, drawn on the 24 by 24 grid with 2 shapes. Its closed shapes also give it a solid style, generated at build time. Tags: switch, off, disabled, setting.

Customize in the icon browser

Sizes, weights and styles

Every glyphfox icon renders along three axes. The stroke grows a little below 24 px so small icons keep their presence.
