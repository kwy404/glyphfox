Luggage tag icon

Luggage tag is an original glyphfox icon from the travel set, drawn on the 24 by 24 grid with 5 shapes. Its closed shapes also give it a solid style, generated at build time. Tags: luggage tag, label, baggage, travel, airport.

Customize in the icon browser

Sizes, weights and styles

Every glyphfox icon renders along three axes. The stroke grows a little below 24 px so small icons keep their presence.
