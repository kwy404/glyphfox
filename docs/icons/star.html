interface

Star icon

Star is an original glyphfox icon from the interface set, drawn on the 24 by 24 grid with one shape. It has a hand drawn duotone layer that takes a second color. Its closed shapes also give it a solid style, generated at build time. Tags: favorite, rating, bookmark, award.

Customize in the icon browser

Sizes, weights and styles

Every glyphfox icon renders along three axes. The stroke grows a little below 24 px so small icons keep their presence.
