commerce

Shopping cart icon

Shopping cart is an original glyphfox icon from the commerce set, drawn on the 24 by 24 grid with 3 shapes. It is an open line drawing, so the solid style keeps the outline look. Tags: cart, buy, checkout, store, basket.

Customize in the icon browser

Sizes, weights and styles

Every glyphfox icon renders along three axes. The stroke grows a little below 24 px so small icons keep their presence.
