social

Brand messenger icon

Brand messenger is an original glyphfox icon from the social set, drawn on the 24 by 24 grid with 2 shapes. It has a hand drawn duotone layer that takes a second color. Its closed shapes also give it a solid style, generated at build time. Tags: messenger, facebook messenger, meta, social, logo, brand.

Customize in the icon browser

Sizes, weights and styles

Every glyphfox icon renders along three axes. The stroke grows a little below 24 px so small icons keep their presence.
