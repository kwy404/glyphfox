glyphfox universe

414,952 open license icons. One API.

Every icon set in Iconify whose license allows redistribution and modification, from Material Symbols, Fluent, Carbon and Font Awesome Free to Lucide, Tabler and Phosphor. One prefix:name resolver, instant search, license metadata on every icon and the glyphfox transforms on top: weight, corners, color, duotone, rotation and motion.

    Sets and licenses

    Every set keeps its own license

    Sets ship only when their license allows redistribution and modification. Every icon carries its license, and the build fails when a set has a missing or non redistributable license. License texts and the notice with every author live in LICENSES.
